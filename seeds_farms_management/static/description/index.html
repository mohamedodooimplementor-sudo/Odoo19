What you get

Farmers management

Farmer data, location and GPS, with a receipts counter per farmer.

Seed receipts

Sequence RSD, contractor and factory weights, difference, sorting and net, with workflow states.

Weighbridge weights

Gross and tare weights give the factory weight automatically.

Moisture deduction

Standard moisture and deduction rate per crop (Corn and Wheat), fully configurable.

Quality grades

Grade A, B and C with bonus or penalty on the price per ardab.

Automated calculations

Number, total weight and number of ardab computed per crop.

Purchase integration

Create a purchase order from a confirmed receipt with the adjusted price.

Inventory integration

Seed weights shown on the incoming receipt.

Settlements

Group receipts into one vendor bill and deduct advance payments.

Season contracts

Agreed quantity and price per farmer, with delivered and remaining ardab tracked on every receipt.

Seasons and fields

Link each receipt to a season and to the farmer's field.

Dashboard

KPIs, monthly weight chart, unsettled amount and contract progress.

PDF receipt ticket

Printable ticket with weights, deductions and signature boxes.

Validation and security

Field checks, delete protection, user and manager groups, multi-company.

Reporting

Pivot and graph analysis by crop, farmer, grade and month.
