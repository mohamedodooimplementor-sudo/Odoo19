Prime Construction Suite

The all-in-one construction ERP for Odoo 19: from tender and BOQ to progress billing, cost control, site operations, quality and safety.

Why contractors choose it

  • One system, full lifecycle: projects, contracts, BOQ, change orders, invoicing, costs, site, quality, risk, and closeout.
  • Real accounting integration: progress invoices with retention, advance payment, withholding tax, social insurance and delay penalties.
  • Cost control that works: actual costs with multi-level threshold approval, overhead allocation, subcontractor AP aging.
  • Field-ready: equipment, daily labor, site diary, and a native Gantt (no Enterprise dependency).
  • Management visibility: interactive dashboard, cash-flow forecast and Earned Value (PV, EV, AC, CPI, SPI).
  • Arabic translation, multi-company rules, dark-mode-aware dashboard, PDF reports without wkhtmltopdf.

What's inside



Dashboard

KPIs, contract vs executed value, alerts and cash-flow forecast in one screen.

Schedule / Gantt and delay analysis

Dynamic approval workflow

Earned Value Management

Pre-construction and tendering

  • Estimates / Quantity Take-off with one-click conversion to a live project.
  • Tenders and bid packages: invite subcontractors, compare bids, award and convert the winner to a subcontractor.
  • Subcontractor prequalification with automatic expiry.
  • Programs / portfolios to group related projects.

Scope, measurement and documents

  • BOQ with reusable templates, cost codes (WBS) and Excel import / export.
  • Measurement Sheets: daily executed quantities, approved and fed automatically into progress invoices.
  • Drawing register with revisions, review workflow and an on-image markup viewer.
  • RFIs, transmittals and a document library with overdue reminders.
  • Resource planning with over-allocation detection and a leveling assistant.

Procurement and inventory

  • Material Requests with approval, then RFQs with a vendor quote matrix and award to Purchase Orders.
  • Purchase Orders from BOQ items, and goods receipts auto-posted as actual costs.
  • Site material issues and returns (to store or to vendor).

Quality, safety and risk

  • Quality: inspection requests (IR / MIR / WIR), NCRs, CAPAs and lab test results.
  • HSE: incidents, work permits, safety inspections, PPE issuance and toolbox talks.
  • Risk register with probability x impact scoring, periodic reviews and a heat map.
  • Compliance: bank guarantees, insurance policies, defects liability period and snagging list.

Performance and reporting

  • KPI Engine: physical completion, cost variance, schedule variance, safety incidents and profit margin, with daily snapshots.
  • PDF reports: project summary, cost analysis, procurement, BOQ, contract summary, client and vendor statements, equipment utilization.
  • Final account (project close-out statement) with retention release tracking.

Arabic, dark mode and security

  • Full Arabic translation and right-to-left layout.
  • Dark mode: the dashboard and risk heat map follow Odoo's dark mode.
  • Organized menu: 9 groups with related screens grouped underneath, so users find things fast.
  • Three roles: Site User, Project Manager and Financial Manager. Prices and costs are hidden from site users.
  • Approval Matrix: approval rules by document type, amount range and role.

Requirements

  • Odoo 19 (Community or Enterprise). Depends on: Project, Accounting, Purchase, Inventory, Employees, Analytic and Discuss (mail).
  • Optional Python libraries on the server: xlsxwriter (Excel export), openpyxl (Excel import), reportlab (PDF reports). A clear message is shown if one is missing.
