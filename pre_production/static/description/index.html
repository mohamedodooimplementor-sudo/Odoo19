Why this module

Factories that fill, pack or process products cannot start a Manufacturing Order before the raw materials are issued, weighed and inspected. Pre-Production Management puts that whole checklist inside Odoo: every step has an owner, a permission, a checklist and an audit trail, and the Manufacturing Order is created only after the last approval.

Key features

  • Created from the Sales Order – flag the product for Pre-Production on the order line and pick its BOM; the Pre-Production Order is created when the order is confirmed.
  • Material & packaging issue – pick the source warehouse and lots, reserve stock and create the issue transfers (auto-validated or validated manually by the stock user).
  • Safe lines – an issued line whose lot is reserved cannot be deleted; its remove button disappears.
  • Weight confirmation – confirm every line (or all at once) before the order moves on.
  • Six configurable quality stages – PRD Line, Product Check 1, Product Check, Filling Line, Pack Line and Product Check 2, each with its own approver group, checklist, rejection reason and re-inspection.
  • Several checks per stage – give a stage more than one template and it opens one inspection for each; the order moves on only when all of them are approved, and a rejection re-opens just the failed one.
  • Smart quality templates – type the inspection points yourself or let them fill in automatically from the order’s raw materials, packaging or finished product.
  • Final step button – Create MO & Final Transfer builds, confirms and finishes the Manufacturing Order with the actual produced quantity (you can edit it at the last stage).
  • Lot traceability – search orders by finished-product lot or by material lot, and print the traceability report.
  • Component status – see at a glance (list, kanban and filters) whether the components are Available, Not Available or already Issued.
  • Activities – users are notified automatically when an order is waiting for their stage.
  • Dashboard – KPIs, charts, filters and the average time spent in every stage, behind its own permission.
  • Reports – a quality report per stage, material issue report, lot traceability report and a final report with production result, inspection details and time per stage.

Step by step

  1. Open Pre-Production → Configuration → Settings and choose the manufacturing warehouse and the workflow requirements.
  2. On each product category, set the warehouses the Pre-Production stock is taken from.
  3. Review the Quality Report Templates and add your own inspection points.
  4. On the user form, open the Pre-Production tab and tick the permissions each person needs.
  5. Create a Sales Order, flag the line for Pre-Production, choose the BOM and confirm it – or create the Pre-Production Order directly.
  6. Press Confirm, then Issue the materials and packaging and confirm the weights.
  7. Each approver opens the order (or the activity assigned to them) and approves or rejects the quality check.
  8. At the last stage set the Actual Produced Quantity and the finished-product lot, then press Create MO & Final Transfer.
  9. Print the Final Report from the order.

Quality templates

Every template line has a type: Written Item, Raw Materials, Packaging Materials or Finished Product. The last three expand automatically into one inspection row per product (product | lot | quantity). Templates only affect new checks – history never changes.

Settings & permissions

  • Manufacturing Warehouse – where the finished product is manufactured.
  • Auto-validate Issue Transfers – validate the issue transfers when Issue is pressed, or leave them for the stock user.
  • Lot Number Required / Material Issue Required / Packaging Issue Required / Weight Confirmation Required – switch workflow steps on or off.
  • Stage Configuration – rename stages, reorder them and choose which are folded in the Kanban view.
  • Permissions – all groups (Manager, Dashboard, Confirm, Issue, Weight, six quality approvals, Final Approval, User) are set from one tab on the user form; ticking Manager ticks everything.

Requirements

Odoo 18 · depends on Sales, Inventory and Manufacturing. Works with lot-tracked and untracked products.
