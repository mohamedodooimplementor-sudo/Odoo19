Up and running in nine steps
1
Open Pre-Production → Configuration → Settings and choose the manufacturing warehouse and the workflow rules.
2
On each product category, tick Pre-Production and choose the warehouses the stock is taken from.
3
Review the Quality Report Templates and add your own inspection points (typed or automatic).
4
On the user form, open the Pre-Production tab and tick each person’s permissions.
5
Create an order (or flag the Sales Order line) and press Confirm.
6
Issue the materials and packaging, then confirm the weights.
7
Each approver gets an activity, opens the order and approves or rejects the quality check.
8
At the last stage set the actual quantity and finished lot, then press Create MO & Final Transfer.
9
Print the Final Report.

Questions
Which Odoo version?
Odoo 18 (Community and Enterprise). It depends on Sales, Inventory and Manufacturing.
Does it work with products without lots?
Yes. Lots are asked only for tracked products, and only for the types (material / packaging) that are marked as required.
Can I change the stage names and order?
Yes. Rename stages, change the sequence and choose which are folded in the Kanban view.
Who can see the costs?
Only users with the Cost permission. For everyone else the fields do not exist, not even in the reports.
Can one stage have more than one inspection?
Yes. Add several templates to the same stage; the stage is complete when every check is approved.

Yes. Bug fixes and updates for the supported version are included. Contact the author below.
