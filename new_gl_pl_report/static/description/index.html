General Ledger and Partner Ledger in dollars, euros and company currency

See every ledger line in the currency your customer, your vendor or your bank actually talks about. This module adds USD, EUR, Combined and Company Currency versions of the General Ledger and the Partner Ledger to Odoo Accounting, and makes the partner form open the right one with a single click.

Why this module

Odoo reports every ledger in the company currency. When you invoice in dollars and euros, you end up converting figures by hand to answer a simple question: how much does this customer owe me in dollars?

This module computes each journal item in a second and a third reporting currency, and gives each currency its own clean report. A dollar report shows dollar columns only. A euro report shows euro columns only. Nothing from the company currency leaks into them.

Key features

  • Four versions of each ledger. USD, EUR, Combined (USD and EUR side by side) and Company Currency.
  • Clean currency reports. The USD and EUR reports show only their own Debit, Credit and Balance columns.
  • Smart button on the partner form. The Partner Ledger button opens this module's report, already filtered on that partner and unfolded.
  • Balance on the button. It shows the partner balance with its sign, positive when the partner owes you and negative when you owe the partner, in the partner's own currency.
  • Picks the report by partner currency. A dollar customer opens the USD report, a euro customer opens the EUR report, anything else opens the Company Currency report.
  • Reporting currencies are yours to choose. Pick them in Accounting settings, per company.
  • Standard Odoo report tools. Date range, journals, analytic filter, partner filter, account type, unreconciled only, draft entries, search bar, unfold all and the PDF and XLSX buttons.
  • Initial balance and running balance in every currency, on both ledgers.

The partner smart button

On the customer or vendor form, the Partner Ledger button now opens this module's report instead of the standard one. The module reads the currencies shown on the partner form (the pricelist currency for customers, the vendor currency for suppliers) and chooses the matching report.

Currency on the partner formReport that opens
Your USD reporting currencyPartner Ledger - USD $
Your EUR reporting currencyPartner Ledger - EUR €
Both of themPartner Ledger - Combined $ + €
Anything else, or nothing selectedPartner Ledger - Company Currency

The button also shows the partner balance (receivable and payable accounts, posted entries). It is expressed in the same currency as the report that opens: dollars for a USD partner, euros for a EUR partner, company currency otherwise. A positive figure means the partner owes you, a negative figure means you owe the partner.

The report opens filtered on that partner, with its lines already unfolded. You can still switch to another version from the report selector.

Configuration

Setup takes less than a minute and is done once per company.

  1. Install the module from Apps.
  2. Make sure the currencies you want to report in are active: Accounting, Configuration, Currencies, and that they have exchange rates.
  3. Open Settings, Accounting and scroll to the Partner Ledger Report section.
  4. In Partner Ledger Currency, choose your dollar currency (normally USD).
  5. In Partner Ledger Euro Currency, choose your euro currency (normally EUR).
  6. Click Save.
SettingWhat it controls
Partner Ledger CurrencyThe currency behind every "$" column and the USD reports. Also decides which partners open the USD report from the smart button.
Partner Ledger Euro CurrencyThe currency behind every "€" column and the EUR reports. Also decides which partners open the EUR report from the smart button.
Set the two currencies before you start using the reports. They are stored per company, so in a multi-company database set them in each company.

How the amounts are calculated

  • If a journal item is already in the reporting currency, its own amount in that currency is used as is.
  • Otherwise the item's company-currency amount is converted at the exchange rate of the entry date, using the rates you maintain in Odoo.
  • Debit and Credit follow the sign of the item, and Balance is a running total that includes the initial balance.
