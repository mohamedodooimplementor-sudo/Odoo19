Why this module?

Expired passports, residence permits and licenses mean fines, blocked travel and operational delays. This module reminds the right people at the right time, inside Odoo and by email, and keeps following up until the document is renewed.

Key features

Three notification channels

Email to the employee, Odoo inbox notification (bell) and a "Document Expiry" activity on the document.

Choose who is notified

Pick the users for each document, or use the defaults: the employee, his manager and the HR managers.

Reminder days per document type

Residence permit at 90 days, work card at 15 days. Set a global default and override it on each document type.

Escalation

If a document is still not renewed close to expiry, the manager, department manager and HR managers are alerted.

Status and dashboard

Valid / Expiring Soon / Expired badges, colored lists, ready filters, and graph and pivot views grouped by department or document type.

One-click renewal

The Renew button creates the new document, archives the old one and closes its pending activities automatically.

Weekly summary for HR

One email listing every document that expired or will expire soon, instead of one email per document.

Editable bilingual email

English / Arabic email template you can edit from Odoo, and a configurable schedule for the scheduled actions.

Quick start

  1. Open an employee and click the Documents button.
  2. Create a document, set the Expiry Date and (optionally) the Notify Users.
  3. Follow everything from Employees → Documents Expiry.
  4. When the employee brings the new document, click Renew.

Configuration

Settings → Employees → Employee Documents Expiry: reminder days, repeat of the expired notice, escalation, weekly summary and the schedule of the scheduled actions. Per document type reminder days are set on the Employee Checklist form.
