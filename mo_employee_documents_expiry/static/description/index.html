Employee Documents Expiry

Keep all employee documents in one place and get warned before they expire.

Why this module?

Expired passports, residence permits and licenses cause fines and operational delays. This module reminds the right people at the right time, in Odoo and by email, until the document is renewed.

Key features

  • Staged reminders: 60, 30, 7, 1 and 0 days before expiry by default. Configurable globally or per document type. Each stage is sent once.
  • Three channels: email to the employee, Odoo inbox notification and a "Document Expiry" activity.
  • Choose who is notified per document, or use the default recipients: the employee, his manager and the HR managers.
  • Escalation to the manager, department manager and HR managers when the document is still not renewed.
  • Weekly summary email for the HR managers.
  • Status and dashboard: Valid / Expiring Soon / Expired, colored lists, filters, graph and pivot views.
  • One-click renewal: creates the new document, archives the old one and closes its activities.
  • Editable bilingual email template (English / Arabic) and a configurable schedule for the scheduled actions.

Configuration

Go to Settings → Employees → Employee Documents Expiry to set the reminder days, expired-notice repeat, escalation, summary email and the schedule of the scheduled actions. Reminder days can also be overridden on each document type (Employee Checklist).

Usage

  1. Open an employee and click the Documents button.
  2. Create a document, set its expiry date and (optionally) the users to notify.
  3. Follow the status from Employees → Documents Expiry.
  4. When the document is renewed, click Renew.
