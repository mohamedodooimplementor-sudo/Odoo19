Employee Documents Expiry
=========================
Keep employee documents in one place and get reminded before they expire:
staged reminders by email, Odoo inbox notifications and activities, escalation,
weekly summary, dashboard and one-click renewal.

Configuration
=============
Settings > Employees > Employee Documents Expiry.
