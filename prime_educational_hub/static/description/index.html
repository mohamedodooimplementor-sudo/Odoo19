The ledger, end to end

Every payment is traceable, from the fee to the receipt

1
Fee
Gross − discount = net. Discounts above a configurable % need Supervisor/Admin sign-off before the fee confirms.
→
2
Installments
Auto-generated on confirm. Late penalties lock in once past a grace period; early-settlement discounts are grantable before payment.
→
3
Payment
Confirmed cash/transfer/card settles the oldest installment first (FIFO). Overpayment becomes usable credit, not a lost balance.
→
4
Credit Reuse
Leftover credit on an old payment is automatically pulled into the next fee's installments — oldest credit, oldest due date first.
→
5
Refund
Approving a refund un-pays the exact installments it funded. Cancelling the refund restores exactly that share — nothing more.
A daily reconciliation check compares every installment paid_amount against every allocation, net of refunds — and pages an admin the moment they drift apart.

Who is it for

Private-lesson centers, tutoring academies, language and training institutes, and small schools that run groups with weekly schedules, collect fees in installments and need to know at any moment who attended, who paid and what is owed. It replaces the mix of spreadsheets, attendance sheets and cash notebooks with one system, and it does not need the Odoo Accounting, HR or Payroll apps.

Fees and money, in detail

  • Fee plans define reusable installment schedules. A fee is gross minus discount; a discount at or above the approval threshold (default 20%) needs Supervisor or Administrator approval before the fee confirms.
  • Late penalties (after a grace period) and early-settlement discounts are configurable by percentage or fixed amount.
  • Payments settle the oldest installment first. Overpayment becomes credit that is reused on the student's next fee.
  • Refunds reverse exactly the installments they funded, and cancelling a refund restores exactly that share.
  • Standalone accounting: expenses with a Draft, Submitted, Approved, Paid workflow, cash and bank accounts with live balances, cash transfers, cashier shifts with daily closing, Income Statement (P&L) and Cash Book.
  • Daily ledger reconciliation alerts the admin if installments and payment allocations ever drift apart.

Configuration guide

Setup takes under an hour for a typical center. Everything lives under the Prime Educational Hub app; the Configuration menu and Settings screen are for Administrators.

Step 1: Settings

Open Configuration > Settings and review the options below.

SettingWhat it does

School's TimezoneGroup schedule times (for example 2:30 PM) are always read in this timezone.
Auto-Generate Sessions X Days AheadA daily job creates upcoming sessions from each group's weekly schedule. Default: 14 days.
Require PIN for Self Check-inStudents scanning the session QR must enter their PIN. On by default.
Default Attendance Status / Exclude Excused AbsencesControls the default mark and whether excused absences count in attendance %.
Default Exam Tie-Break RuleHow ranks are assigned when students have equal scores.
Default Discount Policy and ValuePre-fills the discount on new fees.
Discount Approval Threshold (%)Discounts at or above this percentage need supervisor approval. Default: 20%.
Late Penalty (type, value, grace days)Applied automatically by a daily job after the grace period. Default grace: 3 days.
Early Settlement Discount (type, value, days before due)Granted when an installment is paid early. Default window: 7 days.
Report Language, Header Name, Footer TextApplied to every generated PDF report.
Enable Public Certificate Verification / Student Portal LinksSwitch the public pages on or off. Both on by default.
Dashboard as Home PageUsers with an Education role land on the dashboard after login.
Required Libraries StatusShows whether reportlab, xlsxwriter and qrcode are installed.

Step 2: Required libraries

PDF and Excel reports need reportlab and xlsxwriter, and QR attendance needs qrcode. The module tries to install any missing one with pip when you install or upgrade it, and never blocks the installation if the server has no internet. If a library is reported missing in Settings, install it in the Python environment that runs Odoo and restart the service.

Step 3: Academic setup

Create the structure under Academics > Setup: Academic Years, Terms, Subjects, Levels, Syllabus Topics, Rooms and Branches. Add Holidays under Configuration so sessions are not generated on those days.

Step 4: Money setup

Under Fees & Accounting, create your Fee Plans (installment schedules), check the Payment Methods and Cash / Bank Accounts (Main Cash Drawer and Main Bank Account are created for you), and add Expense Categories. Pipeline Stages for admissions come ready: New, Contacted, Trial Scheduled, Enrolled, Lost.

Step 5: Users and roles

In Settings > Users, give each person one Prime Educational Hub role (see the next section). Link teachers to their user so they can use the teacher portal.

Step 6: Go live

  1. Create Groups with teacher, room, level, capacity and weekly schedule.
  2. Register students and guardians, then enroll them with Quick Enroll (or from Admissions when a lead converts).
  3. Create the fees for each enrollment and confirm them to generate installments.
  4. Open the Dashboard and the Sessions Calendar: upcoming sessions are generated automatically.

Scheduled jobs

Mark overdue installments, apply late penalties, auto-generate upcoming sessions, notify upcoming exams and sessions, auto-mark absent after the attendance window (every 15 minutes), refresh room status (every 5 minutes), refresh teacher-leave warnings, purge old public access logs and run the daily fee ledger reconciliation.

Technical details

  • Odoo 19.0, Community and Enterprise. Depends on base, web, mail and payment. No dependency on Accounting, HR, Payroll or Website.
  • Python libraries: reportlab, xlsxwriter, qrcode.
  • License: Odoo Proprietary License v1.0 (OPL-1).
  • 70+ models, dashboard with clickable KPI cards and charts, light and dark mode.
  • Uninstalling removes all module data (students, groups, fees, payments, certificates). Export what you need first.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need the Odoo Accounting app? No. The module has its own cash and bank accounts, expenses, Cash Book and P&L.

A report or QR code does not work. Check Required Libraries Status in Settings and install any missing library on the server.

Sessions are missing for a date. Open the Sessions Calendar: due sessions are generated automatically from the group schedule. Check the school timezone and holidays too.

Can guardians see their children's data? Yes, through the student link or their login. Turn the links off in Settings if you prefer not to use them.
