A barcode for every unit you buy, stock and sell in

The same product often comes as a bottle, a box and a pallet, but standard Odoo gives it a single barcode. This module lets you give each unit of measure of a product its own barcode. Scan the box barcode and the order gets the product in the unit Box; scan the bottle barcode and it gets Bottle. The barcode then travels with the line through sales, purchasing, inventory and invoicing.

How it works

  • Units of Measure tab on the product form: add a line per unit with its barcode. Every barcode is unique in the database, and a product can have only one barcode per unit.
  • Scan Barcode field at the top of sales orders, purchase orders and transfers (receipts, deliveries, internal). It works while the document is still editable: sales and purchase orders in Draft or Sent, and transfers until they are done or cancelled.
  • A scan adds a line with the right product and unit. Scanning the same barcode again adds one more to that line. An unknown barcode shows a clear message.
  • Barcode (UoM) column on order lines, invoice lines, stock moves and stock move lines. It is copied from the order to the delivery or receipt when the order is confirmed.
  • Print Barcodes on the product form prints a sheet with the name of the product and, for each unit, the unit, its category and a Code 128 barcode.
  • A central list Inventory > Configuration > UoM Barcodes lets you search, filter and group all barcodes by product or unit, and archive the ones you no longer use.

Configuration guide

Step 1: Install

The module needs the Sales, Purchase, Inventory and Accounting apps. There are no extra Python libraries and no settings to fill in.

Step 2: Units of measure

In Inventory > Configuration > Units of Measure, create the units you trade in (Box, Pallet, Carton and similar) in the same category as the product's own unit, with their ratio. A unit from another category is refused.

Step 3: Add the barcodes

Open a product, go to the Units of Measure tab and add one line per unit with its barcode. You can also enter them from Inventory > Configuration > UoM Barcodes.

Step 4: Scan

Click the Scan Barcode field at the top of a sales order, purchase order or transfer and scan with any keyboard-emulating barcode reader, or type the barcode and press Enter.

Good to know

  • Variants: the barcodes belong to the product template. A scan adds the product's first variant, so this module is best for products without several variants.
  • Barcodes are kept apart from Odoo's own product barcode and packaging barcodes: a scan is looked up only in the UoM barcodes of this module.
  • Point of Sale is not included.

Frequently asked questions

Why does Odoo say the barcode already exists? Each barcode can be used only once in the whole database, and each product can have only one barcode per unit.

Why is my unit refused? It must be in the same unit category as the product's unit of measure.

Why is the scan field not showing? On sales and purchase orders it is shown only while the order is a Draft or Sent quotation, and on transfers until they are done or cancelled.

Can I use a scanner? Yes, any barcode reader that types like a keyboard.
